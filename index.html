SAFRA Annual Report FY2025/26: Key Highlights

One year. Many connections. One shared purpose. Explore how SAFRA strengthened commitment to defence, recognised national servicemen’s contributions and supported NSmen and their families in FY2025/26.

SAFRA at a glance

  • Over 417,000 members and an 86% satisfaction rating.
  • Over 1.72 million participations in SAFRA activities.
  • Over 211,000 participations in defence outreach initiatives.
  • Over 75,000 people engaged through Appreciating NSmen initiatives.
  • Over 52,000 participations in cohesion activities for NS units.

Bringing defence closer to the community

NS Experience Camps gave families a taste of NS-inspired activities and a deeper appreciation of their loved ones’ service. Be@SAFRA Open Houses brought families together to learn about the SAF through interactive activities, flight simulators and military K9 encounters.

Commitment to Defence (C2D) Ambassadors from the SAF Veterans’ League shared real-life experiences to reinforce the importance of a strong defence. Total Defence Day commemorative activities equipped participants with practical skills through hands-on demonstrations and interactive games.

Appreciating national servicemen

Through the Appreciating NSmen Art Jam, preschoolers learnt about national servicemen’s contributions and made personalised gifts to thank their fathers for defending Singapore. The Appreciating NSmen Video Competition encouraged students to show their support through creative storytelling.

Collaborations with merchants islandwide secured appreciation deals spanning daily essentials and recreation. SAFRA also gave away complimentary snacks to national servicemen at SAF camps, SAFRA clubs and events.

Strengthening bonds among NSmen, families and friends

National Day celebrations at Marina Bay and SAFRA clubs brought loved ones together for live NDP screenings, performances and nostalgic games. The SG60 SAFRA Family Fiesta featured the SAFRA Diaper Dash and sing-along sessions with Pororo & Friends.

Cohesion events for SAF NS units included archery tag, bowling, inflatable games, water telematches and sports competitions. SAFRA Youth Fiesta brought youths together through Ultimate Frisbee, the DoujinFes Fair and Hololive Official Card Game community workshops. Soundbyte Beer Music Play connected national servicemen and friends through live music and games.

SAFRA Swim for Hope rallied the community to swim for charity and raise funds for the less privileged and those in need.

Membership, made more rewarding

SAFRA membership privileges supported members across food and groceries, education, movies and live entertainment, travel, staycations and local attractions. Exclusive deals made everyday purchases, recreation and time with loved ones more affordable.

Enhancing SAFRA clubs and facilities

Elevated experience at EnergyOne Gym

EnergyOne Gym at SAFRA Toa Payoh was enhanced with redesigned workout zones, new functional training equipment, Booty Builder machines and refreshed amenities to improve members’ fitness experience.

Launch of SAFRA@CMPB

A new café lounge at the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) offered pre-enlistees, national servicemen and MINDEF staff a cosy place to recharge, socialise and learn more about SAFRA.

Sustainability

SAFRA generated 1,074 MWh of solar energy, equivalent to the annual electricity usage of over 320 four-room HDB flats.
